Each place offers a different side of Brasil. Compare what they are best known for, the atmosphere and how long you might want to stay.

Icons, beaches & nightlife

Rio de Janeiro

Rio combines dramatic scenery, famous beaches, nightlife and some of the most recognisable landmarks in Brasil.

  • Best for: First time visitors, nightlife and iconic sights
  • Atmosphere: Energetic and social
  • Suggested stay: 4-6 days

Culture, music & food

Salvador

Salvador combines Afro-Brasilian culture, historic architeture, music and food with a distinctive coastal atmosphere.

  • Best for: Culture, history and music
  • Atmosphere: Colourful and expressive
  • Suggested stay: 3-5 days

Beaches, nature & slow travel

Ilha Grande

Ilha Grande is ideal for travellers who want to slow down, spend time outdoors and enjoy tropical beaches away from Brasil's major cities.

  • Best for: Beaches, nature and relaxation
  • Atmosphere: Laid-back and outdoorsy
  • Suggested stay: 3-4 days

Beaches, social life & outdoors

Florianópolis

Florianópolis combines beaches, outdoor activities and a sociable city atmosphere, making it a good choice for travellers who want variety.

  • Best for: Beaches, social travel and outdoor life
  • Atmosphere: Relaxed but lively
  • Suggested stay: 4-5 days

Need a starting point?

Easy destination combinations

Brasil is huge, so trying to see everything in one trip usually creates more stress than adventure. These simple combinations give first-time visitors a more realistic place to start.

Rio + Ilha Grande

Combine Rio's city energy, landmarks and nightlife with a slower island escape.

Best for: First trips, beaches and variety

8-11 days

Rio + Salvador

Experience two very different cities through iconic sights, Afro Brasilian culture, music, history and food.

Best for: Culture, nightlife and city travel

7-10 days

Florianópolis + Rio

Mix beaches and outdoor living with the energy and famous sights of Rio de Janeiro.

Best for: Social travel, beaches and city life
