The practical basics

Five things to sort before you go

Brasil is a huge country, but a few practical decisions can make your first trip much easier to manage.

01

Getting around

Distances between destinations can be much larger than they look. Plan transport between cities before trying to fit too much into one itinerary.

Think about: Flights, buses, transfers and journey time

02

Money

Plan how you will pay, access cash adn keep a backup payment method available while travelling.

Think about: Cards, cash, exchange rate and fees

03

Staying connected

Reliable mobile data makes maps, transport, bookings and communication much easier while you are moving around.

04

Safety

Different destinations and neighbourhoods require different levels of awareness, especially when carrying valuables or travelling at night.

Think about: Local advice, transport and belongings

05

Language

Portuguese is the main language in Brasil. Learning a few useful phrases can make everyday travel easier and more enjoyable.

Think about: Greeting, directions and ordering food
